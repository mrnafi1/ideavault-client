# IdeaVault 💡

Live Site:https://ideavault-client-dusky.vercel.app

A web platform where innovators share, discover, and validate startup ideas through community interaction.

## Features

- 🚀 **Submit Startup Ideas** — Share your idea with a full form covering title, description, problem, solution, target audience, category, tags, and budget.
- 🔥 **Trending Ideas Feed** — Discover the most popular ideas ranked by community engagement on the homepage.
- 💬 **Comment & Discuss** — Engage with ideas through a full comment system with edit and delete support.
- 🔐 **JWT-Secured Authentication** — Firebase Auth with Google login and email/password, protected by server-side JWT tokens.
- 🌙 **Dark / Light Theme** — Globally toggle between dark and light mode, persisted across sessions.
- 🔍 **Search & Filter** — Find ideas by title or filter by category on the Ideas page.
